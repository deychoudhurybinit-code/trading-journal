TRADING RULEBOOK

1. MINDSET

• NO GREED.

• NO FEAR.

• JUST EXECUTE THE SETUP.

• NO TRADE IS BETTER THAN A LOSS.

• NEVER BREAK THE RULES.

2. SETUP RULE

• NO SETUP = NO TRADE.

• Trade only when the complete setup is present.

• Do not enter just because the market is moving.

• Do not predict or force a trade.

• Do not enter because you are afraid of missing the move.

• Wait for the setup. Then execute.

• A LOSS DOES NOT CREATE A NEW TRADE. ONLY A SETUP CREATES A TRADE.

3. RISK MANAGEMENT

• SL = 5 POINTS.

• 2 SLs IN A DAY → STOP TRADING.

• 40 POINTS EARNED → DAY CLOSED.

• Never increase risk to recover a loss.

• Never move the SL farther away to avoid taking a loss.

4. TRADE LIMIT

• MAXIMUM 3 TRADES PER DAY.

• 3 TRADES IS A MAXIMUM, NOT A TARGET.

• You do NOT have to take 3 trades.

• You can finish the day with 0, 1, 2, or 3 trades.

• NO OVERTRADING.

• NO REVENGE TRADING.

5. BETWEEN-TRADE RULE

• After EVERY trade → WAIT.

• After a losing trade: Mandatory 15-minute gap before another entry.

• No entry during the 15-minute period.

• After 15 minutes, enter ONLY if a valid setup appears.

• After a winning trade: Mandatory 10-minute gap before another entry.

• After 10 minutes, enter ONLY if a valid setup appears.

• NO IMMEDIATE RE-ENTRY.

6. PREVENTING EARLY TRADES

• OLD MISTAKE: Taking trades too early and executing the 3 trades very quickly.

• NEW RULE: DO NOT TRADE BECAUSE YOU HAVE TRADES AVAILABLE.

• Wait for the complete setup.

• Confirm the setup.

• Execute only when the conditions are met.

• If the setup does not appear → DO NOTHING.

• PATIENCE IS PART OF THE STRATEGY.

7. PREVENTING REVENGE TRADING

• OLD MISTAKE: After a loss, immediately taking another trade without a setup to recover the loss.

• NEW RULE: LOSS → STOP → WAIT 15 MINUTES → RESET → LOOK FOR SETUP.

• Never take a trade with the thought: "I need to recover my loss."

• Instead: "My previous trade is finished. I only trade the next valid setup."

8. PREVENTING OVERTRADING

• OLD MISTAKE: Taking additional trades to recover losses or make more profit.

• NEW RULE: Maximum 3 trades per day.

• Never exceed 3 trades.

• Never take an extra trade because you are losing.

• Never take an extra trade because you want more profit.

• Never chase a missed move.

• Never force a setup.

• 3 TRADES = MAXIMUM, NOT A DAILY REQUIREMENT.

9. EXECUTION

• Before entering:

• 1. Is there a valid setup? → If NO: NO TRADE.

• 2. Is the setup confirmed? → If NO: WAIT.

• 3. Am I entering because of greed, fear, FOMO, or revenge? → If YES: NO TRADE.

• 4. Am I within my daily limits? → If NO: STOP.

• 5. If everything is valid: → EXECUTE THE SETUP.

GOLDEN RULES

• NO SETUP = NO TRADE.

• 3 TRADES IS A MAXIMUM, NOT A TARGET.

• A LOSS DOES NOT CREATE A NEW TRADE. ONLY A SETUP CREATES A TRADE.

• NO TRADE IS BETTER THAN A LOSS.

• NEVER BREAK THE RULES.

REMEMBER

• DISCIPLINE > EMOTION

• PATIENCE > IMPULSE

• EXECUTION > PREDICTION

• SETUP > FOMO

• NO TRADE > BAD TRADE

• PROTECT CAPITAL FIRST.

TRADING SETUPS — 1 TO 7

Setup 1 — Moving Average

• 5 SMA

• 9 EMA

• 30 WMA

• Market must be clearly trending.

• If the market is choppy/sideways, do not follow the MA signals.

• If the crossover happens after a big candle without a proper setup → do not enter.

• Wait for the 5-minute candle to close before entering.

• Do not chase if price is already significantly extended from the moving averages.

• Initial stop-loss: 5 points.

• At +5 points: move SL to cost/breakeven.

• Normal setup: approximately 12 points target.

• Breakout of a pattern, support, resistance, or trendline: 20+ points target.

Setup 2 — TD Resistance

• Trendline/resistance must have a minimum of 1 previous test.

• Enter when the TD Resistance setup is confirmed.

• Initial SL: 5 points.

• At +5: SL → cost.

• At +10: SL → +5 points.

• At +15: SL → +10 points.

• At +20: SL → +15 points.

• At +25: SL → +20 points.

• After +15, continue trailing 10 points behind price.

• No fixed target.

• Primary exit is near the resistance.

• If profit reaches 10+ points, the trade can be closed.

Setup 3 — TD Breakout

• Same TD trendline/resistance requirement as Setup 2.

• Trendline/resistance must have a minimum of 1 previous test.

• A breakout of the resistance/trendline is required.

• If a long candle breaks through the level, do not enter immediately.

• Wait for a retest of the broken level.

• Enter only after the retest confirms the breakout.

• No breakout = no Setup 3 trade.

• Initial SL: 5 points.

• At +5: SL → cost.

• At +10: SL → +5 points.

• At +15: SL → +10 points.

• At +20: SL → +15 points.

• At +25: SL → +20 points.

• After +15, continue trailing 10 points behind price.

• No fixed target.

• If resistance is ahead and profit is 10+ points, exit near the resistance.

• Otherwise, continue following the trailing stop-loss.

Setup 4 — Pattern

• Identify a clear pattern/drawing.

• Mark the support and resistance of the drawing.

• At resistance → look for a sell/PE.

• At support → look for a buy/CE.

• Wait for confirmation before entering.

• Risk & Target: To be defined.

Setup 5 — Choppy Market / Box

• Market is moving inside a clear box/range.

• Identify the support and resistance of the box.

• Near resistance → play the resistance side.

• Near support → play the support side.

• Target: 5 points.

• 7 points earned is also enough to close.

• Do not follow the moving-average direction in the choppy market.

• If the box breaks out clearly, stop treating it as a choppy-market trade.

Setup 6 — PE at Resistance / CE at Support

• PE at Resistance.

• CE at Support.

• Initial SL: 5 points.

• At +5 points in the trade's direction → SL to cost/breakeven.

• Target at the next support or resistance.

• At +15 points → start trailing SL by 10 points.

• Continue trailing as price moves further in the trade's direction.

Setup 7 — Candlestick Pattern

• 5-minute chart.

• Identify a clear candlestick pattern.

• Check whether the candlestick pattern is forming near a Support or Resistance.

• Also check whether it is forming near a TD breakout level.

• If the pattern is not near a relevant S/R or TD breakout → do not take the trade.

• Initial SL: 5 points.

• At +5 points → SL to cost/breakeven.

• Target near the relevant Support/Resistance.

• If profit reaches 10+ points, close the trade.

MY FINAL TRADING PRINCIPLE

• My only job is to trade my setups

• and follow my rules.

• I do not need to make money on every trade.
