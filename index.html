TRADING RULEBOOK

1. MINDSET

• NO GREED.

• NO FEAR.

• JUST EXECUTE THE SETUP.

• NO TRADE IS BETTER THAN A LOSS.

• NEVER BREAK THE RULES.

2. SETUP RULE

• NO SETUP = NO TRADE.

• Trade only when the complete setup is present.

• Do not enter just because the market is moving.

• Do not predict or force a trade.

• Do not enter because you are afraid of missing the move.

• Wait for the setup. Then execute.

• A LOSS DOES NOT CREATE A NEW TRADE. ONLY A SETUP CREATES A TRADE.

3. RISK MANAGEMENT

• SL = 5 POINTS.

• 2 SLs IN A DAY → STOP TRADING.

• 40 POINTS EARNED → DAY CLOSED.

• Never increase risk to recover a loss.

• Never move the SL farther away to avoid taking a loss.

4. TRADE LIMIT

• MAXIMUM 3 TRADES PER DAY.

• 3 TRADES IS A MAXIMUM, NOT A TARGET.

• You do NOT have to take 3 trades.

• You can finish the day with 0, 1, 2, or 3 trades.

• NO OVERTRADING.

• NO REVENGE TRADING.

5. BETWEEN-TRADE RULE

• After EVERY trade → WAIT.

• After a losing trade: Mandatory 15-minute gap before another entry.

• No entry during the 15-minute period.

• After 15 minutes, enter ONLY if a valid setup appears.

• After a winning trade: Mandatory 10-minute gap before another entry.

• After 10 minutes, enter ONLY if a valid setup appears.

• NO IMMEDIATE RE-ENTRY.

6. PREVENTING EARLY TRADES

• OLD MISTAKE: Taking trades too early and executing the 3 trades very quickly.

• NEW RULE: DO NOT TRADE BECAUSE YOU HAVE TRADES AVAILABLE.

• Wait for the complete setup.

• Confirm the setup.

• Execute only when the conditions are met.

• If the setup does not appear → DO NOTHING.

• PATIENCE IS PART OF THE STRATEGY.

7. PREVENTING REVENGE TRADING

• OLD MISTAKE: After a loss, immediately taking another trade without a setup to recover the loss.

• NEW RULE: LOSS → STOP → WAIT 15 MINUTES → RESET → LOOK FOR SETUP.

• Never take a trade with the thought: "I need to recover my loss."

• Instead: "My previous trade is finished. I only trade the next valid setup."

8. PREVENTING OVERTRADING

• OLD MISTAKE: Taking additional trades to recover losses or make more profit.

• NEW RULE: Maximum 3 trades per day.

• Never exceed 3 trades.

• Never take an extra trade because you are losing.

• Never take an extra trade because you want more profit
